How a sentence of a report is labelled: what it states of the linked structure (convention of 11 November 2026).

The label lists the claims the sentence makes of the linked structure, joined by "+". It is written from the
whole sentence, never cut, before any reader or model is run on it.

Claims:
- finding: an abnormality of the structure or of one of its parts (a variant counts: an accessory spleen is a
  finding of the spleen). The size of a lesion is part of the finding.
- involved: an abnormality of another structure that touches this one (extends into it, compresses, displaces,
  indents, encircles, destroys a part of it).
- normal: the structure, or something of it, is normal, or something is absent from it.
- measure: a measure of the structure itself (the size of an organ). When the sentence also says it is abnormal
  ("wider than normal", "ectatic", "dilated"), the label is finding+measure.
- history: a fact of its past (a previous exam's finding no longer seen, a known disease, a treatment).
- device: a device in or at it (a tube, a cannula, cement, an implanted valve, sutures).
- not_assessable: it could not be assessed.
- aside: the sentence names the structure only to place something else ("adjacent to", "between", "at the
  level of", "in the neighbourhood of"); nothing of the structure is stated.
- indication: why the exam was asked. procedure: a procedure done on it. nothing: nothing of it.
- uncertain: the writer does not state it as certain, below the level high: "?", "may", "might", "possibly",
  "probably", "suspicious for", "suspected", "in favour of" a hypothesis, "thought to be", "considered",
  "evaluated in the direction of", "can be seen in", "cannot be excluded", "to be evaluated for", alternatives
  joined by "or", a condition ("if there is").
- uncertain_high: stated with an uncertainty of level high: "consistent with", "compatible with", "in line
  with" (Panicek and Hricak 2016: more than 90%). "May be compatible with" is uncertain, not uncertain_high.

Not an uncertainty (see the reading manual): "nonspecific", "cannot be characterized" (the nature is open);
"as far as can be observed" (the exam's limit); "slight", "faint", "-like", "approximately" (approximators);
"according to", "it was understood that" (a source); "stable", "new", "decreased" (time); "secondary to",
"due to", "in favor of the heart" (a stated cause or attribution).

A sentence of the impression that repeats a finding is labelled as the finding. The structure is the linked
class: a part of it (a segment, a lobe's lingula, a valve of the heart) is the structure.
